p.124, last paragraph

The idea behind the analysis is really based on a binomial test, not a sign test, as the text says. What we want to calculate is the same as the probability of getting "tail" 6 times of out of 6 trials of coin toss, assuming H0: p=.05. This probability can be calculated as:

nCr*p*q (where nCr=the number of combinations of choosing r out of n; p=0.5; q=1-0.5=0.5)

Therefore:

nCr*p*q = 6C6*p^6*(1-p)^(6-6) 

Since 6C6 = 1 (there is one way to choose 6 elements from the pool of 6 elements), and (1-p)^(6-6)=0.5^0=1,

nCr*p*q = 6C6*p^6*(1-p)^(6-6) = p^6=0.5^6<.05
